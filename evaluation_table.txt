The Week 9 RAGAS pipeline always runs a single, fixed retrieve() call using the raw query, regardless of what the question actually needs. It has no capacity to perform multiple iterations on the same query.

The Week 10 ReAct agent instead decided how to handle the complex bat/bee question and broke it up into three separate self-written sub-queries (seen in the tool_calls list). 
It then judged, on its own, that it had enough information to stop (shown by ‘Iterations: 2), where iteration 1 was the model choosing to retrieve and iteration 2 was it choosing to answer rather than search again.
